Important Info

Built for the Blind and Visually Impaired community, XB-Focus provides a private, local AI experience powered by Gemma 2 2B, helping users chat, understand documents, and stay productive without relying on the cloud. Running entirely on your device through Ollama, it requires Windows 10/11 (64-bit), an Intel Core i5 (10th Gen+) or AMD Ryzen 5 processor, 8 GB RAM (16 GB recommended), approximately 8 GB of available SSD storage, and no dedicated GPU. Note: Please download and install Ollama before getting the ZIP file.
